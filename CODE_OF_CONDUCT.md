# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want FARMAN to be a welcoming project for people of every background,
language and experience level. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make
participation harassment-free, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable technical feedback about code, not people.
- Respect different scripts, languages, keyboard layouts and accessibility
  needs.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully and apologize when you cause harm.
- Credit other people's work and follow the project's license terms.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory comments or imagery, including about language, ethnicity,
  religion, gender or disability.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately shipping malware, telemetry or license-incompatible assets.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the contact
details on <https://shahbazteam.ir> or open a confidential security advisory if
the issue also involves a vulnerability. Reports will be handled discreetly.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for behaviour that violates this code.
